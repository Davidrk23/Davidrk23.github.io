about

Short bio

I'm a third-year PhD candidate advised by Prof. A. Mentor in the Systems & Learning Group. My research sits at the boundary of machine learning and systems: I care about why models fail once they're deployed, and how to make training and inference cheaper without quietly trading away robustness.

Before the PhD I completed my BSc at Example University, where I worked on distributed training. Outside research I help organize our department's reading group and mentor undergraduates getting into research.
